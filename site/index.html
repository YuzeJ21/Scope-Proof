Bounded evaluation

Evaluate locally; report a reproducible problem

ScopeProof is an acceptance-coverage assistant, not an AI code reviewer or correctness oracle. Stage 1 is closed as not pursued, with every external-evidence count at zero. Owner-led Stage 2 maintains a bounded engineering reference product. Stage 3 is dormant and gated. No paid product or billing is active.

Evaluation downloads require no public case, qualifying review, or participant feedback. Optional design-partner discovery remains inactive until separately authorized; a download does not authorize outreach or establish customer validation.

Likes, views, stars, impressions, downloads, constructed demos, and automated checks are not product validation.

In the archived or separately authorized participant path, genuine feedback requires a completed review and a participant-selected outcome: a useful previously unknown gap, already-known information, or material product friction. Incomplete reviews receive no completed-review credit. Alpha feedback remains an explicit local opt-in; Standard reviews create no research record.

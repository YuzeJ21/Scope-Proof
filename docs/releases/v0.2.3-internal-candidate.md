# ScopeProof v0.2.3 internal candidate

Status: Internal only; not published  
Track: Evidence Quality  
Validation credit: zero

ScopeProof v0.2.3 explains why deterministic evidence retrieval did or did not
produce candidates, makes changed-review decisions easier to revisit, and
classifies the completed R-002 misses without changing the core retrieval or
gate rules.

This candidate is engineering evidence only. It does not prove correctness,
runtime behavior in a target repository, customer demand, accessibility
conformance, or product validation, and it does not advance Stage 1.

## Included

- Validated per-criterion retrieval outcomes and diagnostics.
- Backward-compatible review persistence: historical records do not invent
  diagnostics that were never captured.
- Diagnostics in the workbench, CLI-created reviews, constructed demo, and
  JSON, Markdown, CSV, and HTML exports.
- R-002 miss taxonomy with 14 threshold-rejection cases and one unsupported
  evidence-form case. The frozen inputs and result were not rescored.
- Evidence-delta comparison guidance and an explicit list of prior decisions
  requiring re-review.
- Compact candidate-strength and unresolved-criteria summaries.
- A visible keyboard-focus treatment plus a bounded macOS first-use and
  accessibility engineering audit.

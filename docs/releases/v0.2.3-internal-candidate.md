ScopeProof v0.2.3 explains why deterministic evidence retrieval did or did not
produce candidates, makes changed-review decisions easier to revisit, and
classifies the completed R-002 misses without changing the core retrieval or
gate rules.

This candidate is engineering evidence only. It does not prove correctness,
runtime behavior in a target repository, customer demand, accessibility
conformance, or product validation, and it does not advance Stage 1.

## Included

- Validated per-criterion retrieval outcomes and diagnostics.
- Backward-compatible review persistence: historical records do not invent
  diagnostics that were never captured.
- Diagnostics in the workbench, CLI-created reviews, constructed demo, and
  JSON, Markdown, CSV, and HTML exports.
- R-002 miss taxonomy with 14 threshold-rejection cases and one unsupported
  evidence-form case. The frozen inputs and result were not rescored.
- Evidence-delta comparison guidance and an explicit list of prior decisions
  requiring re-review.
- Compact candidate-strength and unresolved-criteria summaries.
- A visible keyboard-focus treatment plus a bounded macOS first-use and
  accessibility engineering audit.
- Immutable runtime-evidence IDs and repository, PR, and exact-head provenance
  for new E3/E4 records and their paired manual decisions.
- Deterministic version 1/2 to version 3 migration that preserves legacy history
  without inventing resolution links; affected decisions require reconfirmation.
- Runtime identity and legacy-unlinked state in the workbench and all four
  validated export formats.
- Visible skipped-CI limitation copy and bundle-less criterion-draft recovery
  without changing deterministic gate semantics.
- Immutable criteria-source snapshots containing source URI, optional revision,
  exact source-text and normalized-criteria digests, confirmer, and timestamp.
- Version 4 persistence with conservative version 1–3 migration that invents no
  criteria provenance and requires reconfirmation before eligibility returns.
- Criteria-source provenance in CLI and advanced Action review flows, local
  alpha records, the workbench, and JSON, Markdown, CSV, and HTML exports.
- A less dense workbench and public-alpha hierarchy using supported theme
  controls and progressive disclosure without removing labels, safety copy,
  focus treatment, or evidence boundaries.
